Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Seasonia ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile app for seasonal, moon, and sun-time tracking.

Our core philosophy is Privacy by Design. We believe that your time, location, and seasonal journey should remain yours. This Privacy Policy explains what data Seasonia accesses, how it is used, and your choices. By using Seasonia, you agree to this policy.

2. Data We Access and Process

Seasonia is designed to run entirely offline for its core functionality. We do not even request the INTERNET permission in our app manifest.

a) Location (Optional)

Seasonia may request location permission to improve sunrise and sunset accuracy for your exact coordinates.

  • Permissions used: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • Purpose: Astronomical calculations (sunrise/sunset, moon phases, and related seasonal view accuracy).
  • Processing: All calculations are performed on-device.
  • Storage: Your last known latitude, longitude, and an optional location label (retrieved via the system Geocoder) are stored locally in app preferences.
  • Geocoding: Since Seasonia does not have Internet access, it relies on the Android system's built-in Geocoding service to resolve coordinates into human-readable city names when possible.

If you deny location permission, Seasonia still works using timezone-based approximate calculations.

b) Local App Settings and State

Seasonia stores app settings locally on your device. This data includes:

  • Selected language and theme preferences.
  • App usage statistics (e.g., launch count to determine when to show the review prompt).
  • Review prompt state (cooldown and completion status).
  • Last location values (if permission was granted).
  • Home screen widget configuration and state.

c) Widgets

Seasonia provides home screen widgets. If you add widgets, Android stores the widget placement and state on your device. Seasonia can refresh widgets on system events (such as time, date, timezone or locale changes and device boot). These refreshes occur locally.

d) In-app Review

Seasonia uses the Google Play In-App Review API to request ratings. The display of the review dialog is controlled entirely by Google Play.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Network data: Because we do not request the INTERNET permission, our app cannot send your data to any remote server.
  • Personal identifiers: We do not require account sign-ups. We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number. If you contact us by email, we receive only the information you include in your message and use it solely to respond to you.
  • Analytics and tracking: Seasonia does not include Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, advertising SDKs, or any crash-reporting SDKs that would transmit data from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Seasonia uses Google Play services for platform-level features:

  • In-App Review

These services are part of the Android ecosystem and may process data according to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data. All app data stays on your device.
  • Limited data processing may occur by platform providers (e.g., Google Play Services) only when their specific features, such as in-app reviews, are triggered.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Seasonia has no user accounts and no server-side data. Local data is kept on your device until:

  • You clear the app's data.
  • You uninstall the app.
  • You change or reset values in the app settings.

7. Security

We follow best practices for local data storage on Android. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

Seasonia is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example, by email), please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

9. Your Choices

You can:

  • Deny location permission and use approximate calculations instead.
  • Change language and settings at any time in the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all associated local data.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the right to request access to your data, request its deletion, or object to its processing. Because Seasonia stores data only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. For any other request, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted within the app and/or on this page with a new update date.
